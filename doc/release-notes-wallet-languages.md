GUI changes
-----------

- The wallet no longer offers eight languages it inherited from Dash: Arabic, Bulgarian, Dutch,
  Finnish, Romanian, Slovak, Thai and Vietnamese. Nobody maintained them: almost half to more than
  half of the wallet's text was missing in each, so the wallet showed a mix of that language and
  English. Where one of them was selected, or is the language of the computer, the wallet's own text
  is now in English; standard dialogs drawn by Qt can still follow the computer's language. English,
  German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese stay, Italian,
  Polish, Turkish and Traditional Chinese are back, and Ukrainian, Indonesian and Hungarian are new.
  The wallet's translations are maintained in the DeFCoN Transifex project
  (https://app.transifex.com/defcon/defcon/); a removed language can come back once it is translated
  there.
