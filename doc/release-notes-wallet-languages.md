GUI changes
-----------

- The wallet no longer offers twelve languages it inherited from Dash: Arabic, Bulgarian, Dutch,
  Finnish, Italian, Polish, Romanian, Slovak, Thai, Turkish, Vietnamese and Traditional Chinese.
  Nobody maintained them: about a third to more than half of the wallet's text was missing in each,
  so the wallet showed a mix of that language and English. Where one of them was selected, or is
  the language of the computer, the wallet's own text is now in English; standard dialogs drawn by
  Qt can still follow the computer's language. English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese,
  Russian, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese stay. Translations are maintained in the DeFCoN
  Transifex project (https://app.transifex.com/defcon/defcon/), and a removed language can come
  back once it is translated there.
